Tallinn, Estonia · Filmmaker

Creating worlds by hand. Writing, modeling, designing.

Bachelor's degree from Baltic Film, Media and Arts School focused on production design. Author of several worldwide festival short-listed and awarded films. Master's degree underway from Tallinn University focused on game design & narrative.

Based in Tallinn, EST Originally from Zagreb, CRO VilimLackoCug@proton.me
Scroll

Since 2016 I have chased creative work. Majoring in media technician at School for Art, Design, Graphics and Clothes Zabok. Followed by a 6 month course in Blender and 3D game art. My education led me to pursue an undergraduate degree from BFM under the creative module, to a graduate degree in learning game design from TLU.

Since 2022 I have been based in Tallinn. His short Memory Merchant, shot as a BFM thesis, was selected for EnergaCAMERIMAGE's Film & Art School Etudes Competition and PÖFF Shorts. Alongside film sets, I've spent years crossing Europe with Erasmus+ youth exchanges — participating in projects, leading interviews, filming and editing the journey.
